Definitions

Water year
The rain season, from Oct 1 to Sep 30. For example, 2026-27 runs from Oct 1, 2026 to Sep 30, 2027.
Season to date
Rain since Oct 1 of the current water year.
Normal to date
The average rain from Oct 1 through the same calendar date, across past water years with complete data (at least 95% of days). The current water year is never included. Each gauge shows how many years its average uses.
Same date last year
Rain in the previous water year from Oct 1 through the same calendar date.
% of average season
Season to date as a share of the average total for a whole water year.
Limited history
Shown instead of an average when a gauge has fewer than 5 complete water years.
Times
PST (Pacific Standard Time) all year, as reported by the district.

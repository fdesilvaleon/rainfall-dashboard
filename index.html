Definitions

Water year
The rain season, from Oct 1 to Sep 30. For example, 2026-27 runs from Oct 1, 2026 to Sep 30, 2027.
Season to date
Rain since Oct 1 of the current water year.
Normal to date
The average rain from Oct 1 through the same calendar date, across past water years with complete data (at least 95% of days). The current water year is never included. Each gauge shows how many years its average uses.
Same date last year
Rain in the previous water year from Oct 1 through the same calendar date.
% of average season
Season to date as a share of the average total for a whole water year.
Trail conditions
A rough guide for running and biking (gravel included) in the selected trail region, from the average of that region's gauges (pick the region on the trail conditions map or with the Region menu). Each time block (morning 4-10 AM, mid day 10 AM-4 PM, afternoon 4-10 PM, Pacific time) gets the worse of three checks. Ground wetness: rain in the 24 hours before the block, plus half of the rain 24-48 hours before, plus a quarter of the rain 48-72 hours before (measured rain, then forecast rain for hours not measured yet). Running: under 0.10 in optimal, under 0.50 good, under 1.00 fair, otherwise poor. Biking is stricter: under 0.02 optimal, under 0.05 good, under 0.15 fair, otherwise poor. Only gauges with data for all 72 hours are averaged (the number used shows when you hover over or tap a rating). Season adjustment: early in the water year the dry soil absorbs the first storms, so the ground-wetness limits are multiplied while the season has been dry. The season total is the average of those gauges' rain since Oct 1; under 2.0 in, biking's limits are 2.5 times the above; 2.0 to under 5.0 in, 1.5 times; 5.0 in or more, the limits above. Running isn't adjusted (1.0 at every step). If the season total isn't known, no adjustment is made. A line under the section says when it applies. Rain during the block (forecast): under 0.01 in optimal; 0.01-0.15 in good for running and fair for biking; more than 0.15 in poor. Comfort, from the forecast "feels like" temperature (which already includes wind chill and heat index): running optimal 45-68°F, good 38-45 or 68-78, fair 30-38 or 78-88, poor outside that; biking optimal 50-72°F, good 42-50 or 72-82, fair 34-42 or 82-92, poor outside that. At an exact boundary the better rating applies. Today's big rating is the worst of the blocks still ahead; blocks that have ended are marked "Past". The Valley floor is pavement, so ground wetness isn't used there: each block gets the worse of rain during the block and comfort, and is at best fair for 12 hours after 0.02 in of rain and poor for 6 hours after 0.15 in.
Last 7 days and next 7 days
Solid bars: rain measured by the gauge on each of the last 7 days, added up from the hourly data, midnight to midnight in PST (the district's time). Striped bars: the National Weather Service's expected rain for the 2.5 km area around the gauge, for today and the next 6 days (Pacific local calendar days, updated about every hour), with the highest chance of rain that day. Today shows both: rain measured so far, and the forecast for the whole day. For past days, a striped bar next to the measured bar shows the last forecast NWS issued the day before (1 day ahead), from the saved forecasts, so the two can be compared. Days with no saved day-before forecast show the measured bar only.

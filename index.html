Definitions

Water year
The rain season, from Oct 1 to Sep 30. For example, 2026-27 runs from Oct 1, 2026 to Sep 30, 2027.
Season to date
Rain since Oct 1 of the current water year.
Normal to date
The average rain from Oct 1 through the same calendar date, across past water years with complete data (at least 95% of days). The current water year is never included. Each gauge shows how many years its average uses.
Same date last year
Rain in the previous water year from Oct 1 through the same calendar date.
% of average season
Season to date as a share of the average total for a whole water year.
Last 7 days and next 7 days
Solid bars: rain measured by the gauge on each of the last 7 days, added up from the hourly data, midnight to midnight in PST (the district's time). Striped bars: the National Weather Service's expected rain for the 2.5 km area around the gauge, for today and the next 6 days (Pacific local calendar days, updated about every hour), with the highest chance of rain that day. Today shows both: rain measured so far, and the forecast for the whole day. For past days, a striped bar next to the measured bar shows the last forecast NWS issued the day before (1 day ahead), from the saved forecasts, so the two can be compared. Days with no saved day-before forecast show the measured bar only.
Limited history
Shown instead of an average when a gauge has fewer than 5 complete water years.
Times
PST (Pacific Standard Time) all year, as reported by the district.
